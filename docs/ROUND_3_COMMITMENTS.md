# Continuity AI — Round 3 Development Commitments

## Introduction

For Round 3, the team commits to improving Continuity AI's reliability, transparency, source intelligence, data protection and usability. The following enhancements are planned future improvements and are not claimed as completed Round 2 features.

## Commitment 1: Intelligent Repair-and-Retest Loop

**Objective:** Make incomplete or unreliable project knowledge easier to identify, correct and verify.

Planned improvements:
- Identify missing, incomplete or conflicting information in project knowledge.
- Suggest relevant evidence and supporting sources to resolve gaps.
- Introduce multilingual and context-aware language-model extraction to improve understanding of project documents.
- Improve evidence matching using exact matching, normalized text matching and confidence-based fuzzy matching.
- Send uncertain matches for human review rather than automatically marking them as verified.
- Allow managers to correct information, generate an updated handover and rerun validation tests.

**Expected outcome:** More reliable knowledge extraction, better evidence verification and fewer unresolved information gaps.

## Commitment 2: Decision Timeline, Version History and Acknowledgement

**Objective:** Improve transparency and accountability throughout project handovers.

Planned improvements:
- Introduce a chronological decision timeline.
- Maintain version history for handover documents.
- Record important changes, reviews, approvals and responsible users.
- Allow incoming team members to acknowledge receipt of a handover.
- Preserve a traceable record of important project decisions.

**Expected outcome:** Better accountability, traceability and continuity when project responsibilities change.

## Commitment 3: Source Expansion and Data Protection

**Objective:** Support more document formats while strengthening information security.

Planned improvements:
- Add DOCX document support.
- Add task-export CSV import.
- Introduce OCR processing for scanned PDF documents.
- Detect potentially sensitive information, including secrets and personal data.
- Provide automatic detection and redaction workflows with human review.
- Add PDF export for approved handover documents.

**Expected outcome:** Broader document compatibility and safer knowledge sharing.

## Additional Cross-Cutting Improvement: Cleaner and More Intuitive UI

**Objective:** Make Continuity AI easier to understand and use for managers and incoming team members.

Planned improvements:
- Improve visual consistency across application screens.
- Simplify navigation and dashboard layouts.
- Establish clearer information hierarchy.
- Improve responsive layouts for different screen sizes.
- Use consistent status indicators and accessible interface components.
- Provide clearer feedback for errors, conflicts, review progress and handover status.

**Expected outcome:** A cleaner, more professional and user-friendly experience that reduces confusion during project knowledge transfer.

## Commitment and Scope

These improvements are proposed for Round 3. The team will prioritize them based on technical feasibility, user feedback, reliability and security requirements. Existing Round 2 functionality will remain the baseline for future development.
